IDEA 4: The Upkeep & Supply Drain Calculator
----------------------------------------
What it does: A major trap for players is buying an item without realizing how expensive
it is to run. This feature calculates the real-time cost of ammunition, runes, and item
charges (e.g., Scythe blood fractions, Shadow soul charges, or Toxic Blowpipe scales)
consumed per minute of continuous combat, showing the net profit of a gear setup.

Difficulty: Medium
Requires pulling ammunition/charge usage data and cross-referencing live GE prices for
components.

Who benefits most: Mid-game players stepping into high-tier content.

IDEA 5: "Next Max Hit" Combat Level Simulator
---------------------------------------------
What it does: Tied to your Wise Old Man stats pull, this feature tells players exactly
how many combat levels (e.g., Strength, Ranged, or Magic) they are away from unlocking
their next "max hit" with their current gear. It removes the guesswork out of deciding
whether to pause bossing to go train at NMZ or crabs for an afternoon.

Difficulty: Easy
Calculated by incrementing the player's base stats in your existing formulas until the
damage output integer increases.

Who benefits most: Beginners and Mid-game players.

IDEA 9: TTK to GP/Hour Profit Predictor
----------------------------------------
What it does: Abstract DPS values are translated into expected real-world time and gold.
By taking the boss's total Hitpoints, factoring in average respawn/banking times, and
querying the wiki's live average drop-table value, the app tells the user: "With your
current setup, you can make roughly 1.8M GP/hour here; upgrading to the recommended
necklace boosts that to 2.1M GP/hour."

Difficulty: Medium
Requires math that factors in boss health, defense variables, and expected kill pacing
against live market drop data.

Who benefits most: All players.

IDEA 10: The "Dry-Streak" Grind Clock
--------------------------------------
What it does: A psychological preparation tool that pairs with drop-rate calculations.
Based on the player's calculated Time-to-Kill (TTK) for a specific boss, it estimates
the exact number of hours of continuous combat it will take them to hit the drop rate
for a signature item (like a Bow of Faerdhinen or a Voidwaker piece), alongside a
simulator that shows how likely they are to go dry.

Difficulty: Medium
Requires running a fast, client-side statistical probability loop alongside your
calculated time metrics.

Who benefits most: Ironmen and Drop-grinders.
